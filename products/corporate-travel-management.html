Source

Get the best transient rates quickly and easily. Source LBT! Sourcing saves time and money searching, bidding and selecting from over 300,000 hotels for your transient travel programs.

Audit

Ensure accurate rates and availability. Rate Parity up-to-date & accurate confirms your negotiated hotel rates are loaded accurately and bookable in every global distribution system.

Engagements and projects

Hotel directory

Rate parity

Compare your negotiated rates across commercial channels (Travelocity, Orbitz, etc) to ensure you're getting competitive rates in the marketplace.

Rate availability

Proactively check the availability of your negotiated rates to ensure they're available at the correct rate.

Reverse audit
